## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The PulseWell Digital Intake & Client Experience Portal is a consumer-facing digital platform designed to streamline client onboarding, health readiness assessment, and personalized wellness journey tracking. This portal will serve as the primary touchpoint for new and existing clients seeking non-clinical exercise readiness evaluation and tailored wellness guidance.

The project aims to enhance the client experience through an intuitive digital interface while maintaining industry-leading privacy standards and data protection compliance. By digitizing the intake process and providing real-time progress monitoring, PulseWell will improve operational efficiency, increase client engagement, and establish a scalable foundation for future wellness service expansion.

## BUSINESS TARGETS

### BT-01: Client Onboarding Efficiency
**Objective:** Reduce average client intake process time from manual paper-based methods to a digital workflow.  
**Target:** Achieve 70% reduction in intake completion time (target: < 15 minutes per client)  
**Success Metric:** Average intake session duration and completion rate tracking

### BT-02: Exercise Readiness Assessment Accuracy
**Objective:** Implement a scientifically-informed assessment framework for non-clinical exercise readiness evaluation.  
**Target:** Achieve 95% consistency in readiness categorization across assessment interactions  
**Success Metric:** Inter-rater reliability score and assessment outcome consistency analysis

### BT-03: Client Engagement & Retention
**Objective:** Increase client engagement through personalized wellness journey tracking and progress visualization.  
**Target:** Achieve 80% active user engagement within 30 days of portal access  
**Success Metric:** Monthly active users (MAU), session frequency, feature adoption rates

### BT-04: Data Privacy & Security Compliance
**Objective:** Establish and maintain compliance with consumer privacy standards and data protection regulations.  
**Target:** Achieve 100% compliance with CCPA, GDPR, and HIPAA-adjacent standards applicable to wellness data  
**Success Metric:** Security audit results, privacy impact assessment scores, zero data breach incidents

### BT-05: Scalability & Performance
**Objective:** Design a platform capable of supporting growth from initial launch through multi-market expansion.  
**Target:** Support 10,000+ concurrent users with < 2-second average page load time  
**Success Metric:** System uptime (99.9%), average response time, concurrent user capacity testing

### BT-06: Integration with Existing Systems
**Objective:** Seamlessly integrate the portal with current CRM, billing, and health tracking systems.  
**Target:** Achieve bi-directional data synchronization with 99.5% accuracy  
**Success Metric:** Data reconciliation reports, system integration audit logs

### BT-07: Client Satisfaction & NPS Growth
**Objective:** Deliver exceptional client experience that increases Net Promoter Score (NPS).  
**Target:** Achieve minimum NPS score of +50 within first 90 days post-launch  
**Success Metric:** Post-interaction surveys, NPS tracking, qualitative feedback analysis

## PROJECT SCOPE

### In Scope

1. **Digital Intake Module**
   - Multi-step questionnaire for client onboarding
   - Secure data collection for personal information and wellness preferences
   - Real-time form validation and error handling

2. **Exercise Readiness Assessment Engine**
   - Proprietary assessment algorithm based on evidence-informed exercise physiology
   - Scoring matrix and readiness level categorization (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced)
   - Personalized recommendations based on assessment outcomes

3. **Client Dashboard**
   - Personal wellness profile and goal tracking
   - Progress visualization (charts, metrics, achievements)
   - Personalized exercise recommendations and resource library

4. **Privacy & Security Infrastructure**
   - End-to-end encryption for sensitive data transmission
   - Role-based access control (RBAC)
   - Audit logging and compliance reporting tools

5. **Integration Layer**
   - APIs for CRM system integration
   - Billing system data synchronization
   - Third-party wellness platform connectors (as applicable)

6. **User Support & Documentation**
   - In-app help system and FAQ
   - Client-facing user guides and tutorials
   - Administrator documentation

## SCOPE EXCLUSIONS

### EX-001: Clinical Diagnosis & Medical Treatment
**Excluded:** The portal shall NOT provide clinical diagnoses, medical prescriptions, or treatment recommendations. All assessments are strictly non-clinical in nature and do not constitute medical advice.

### EX-002: Telehealth & Remote Medical Consultation
**Excluded:** This project does NOT include telehealth services, virtual consultations with healthcare providers, or medical practitioner interactions. Client support is limited to digital platform assistance only.

### EX-003: Prescription & Medication Management
**Excluded:** The portal does NOT include functionality for prescription management, medication ordering, pharmacy integration, or medication interaction checking.

### EX-004: Advanced Biometric Monitoring Integration
**Excluded:** Direct integration with medical-grade biometric devices (e.g., ECG monitors, blood pressure devices, clinical-grade wearables) is NOT included in this phase. Consumer-grade fitness trackers may be supported in future phases pending technical evaluation.

### EX-005: Genomic & Genetic Data Processing
**Excluded:** The platform does NOT collect, process, or provide insights based on genetic or genomic data. Family health history may be collected for context only, without genetic analysis.

### EX-006: Mental Health & Psychological Assessment
**Excluded:** Psychological evaluation, mental health screening, or psychiatric assessment is NOT included. The portal focuses exclusively on physical wellness and exercise readiness.

### EX-007: Regulatory Approval for Clinical Claims
**Excluded:** Obtaining FDA approval, medical device classification, or clinical efficacy validation is NOT within the project scope. The portal is positioned as a consumer wellness tool, not a medical device.

### EX-008: Multi-Language Support (Phase 1)
**Excluded:** While future internationalization is planned, Phase 1 launch will support English language only. Localization for additional languages will be addressed in subsequent project phases.

### EX-009: Offline Functionality & Native Mobile Apps
**Excluded:** Phase 1 deployment focuses on web-based responsiveness only. Standalone native mobile applications (iOS/Android) and offline-first functionality are OUT OF SCOPE for initial release and will be evaluated for Phase 2.
