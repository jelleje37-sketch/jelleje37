## Stakeholder Engagement & Communication Strategy

### Engagement Levels

| Level | Definition | Stakeholders |
|-------|-----------|--------------|
| **Lead/Manage** | Actively involved in decision-making and execution | ES, SOD, LM, FDS, PT/GFI, CL, LPR, PM |
| **Collaborate** | Provide input, review deliverables, attend key meetings | PT/GFI, SSV, PD, DRA |
| **Inform** | Receive regular updates and status communications | All stakeholders |
| **Support** | Provide functional expertise as needed | All stakeholders |

### Communication Frequency

- **Executive Steering Committee:** Monthly or as-needed
- **Project Core Team:** Weekly
- **Extended Stakeholders:** Bi-weekly status updates
- **Ad-hoc:** Issue escalations and change requests (within 24 hours)

## SECTION 2: RACI MATRIX

### RACI Definition

- **R (Responsible):** Performs the work; does the actual execution
- **A (Accountable):** Has final authority and approval; makes decisions
- **C (Consulted):** Provides input, expertise, and feedback before execution
- **I (Informed):** Kept updated on progress and completion; receives notifications

## KEY GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES

✅ **Clear Accountability:** Every work package has one and only one Accountable (A) stakeholder  
✅ **Balanced Responsibility:** R assignments are distributed across multiple stakeholders to enable collaboration  
✅ **Executive Oversight:** Executive Sponsor (ES) retained decision authority on all major work packages  
✅ **Client Voice:** Client Leadership (CL) has strong input and approval authority on user-facing deliverables  
✅ **Privacy & Compliance:** Legal & Compliance (LM) and Legal & Privacy Regulator (LPR) maintain high involvement in all compliance-related activities  
✅ **Technical Leadership:** Product/Technical Architect (PT/GFI) provides technical guidance and approval on all technical work packages
