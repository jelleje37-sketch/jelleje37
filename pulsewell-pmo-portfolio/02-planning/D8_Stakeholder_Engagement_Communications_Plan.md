## DOCUMENT OVERVIEW

This Stakeholder Engagement and Communications Plan defines how the project team will engage stakeholders, communicate progress, manage expectations, and collect actionable feedback throughout planning, design, implementation, testing, and launch. The plan supports governance alignment and ensures that each stakeholder receives the correct information at the appropriate cadence and in the right format.

**Purpose:**
- Maintain alignment with sponsors, executives, and operational stakeholders
- Provide transparent project status and decision support
- Manage change expectations and reduce delivery risk
- Create consistent feedback loops to support course correction and quality assurance

---

## 1. STAKEHOLDER ENGAGEMENT STRATEGY

### 1.1 Engagement Objectives

- Keep business stakeholders informed of progress against requirements and design decisions

## 3. COMMUNICATION CONTENT DESIGN

### 3.1 Standard Status Report Template
Each status report should include:
- Overall project status (green / amber / red)
- Scope status and completed milestones
- Schedule variance and key dependencies
- Budget status and variance to baseline
- Risks, issues, and actions
- Decisions required from sponsor or governance board
- Next key milestones and upcoming actions

### 3.2 Executive Summary Format
- 1-page dashboard covering major project metrics
- Highlight key achievements over last reporting period
- Identify top 3 risks and decisions needed
- Include budget and schedule health indicator
- Provide clear recommendation for leadership action

### 3.3 Team-Level Communication Format
- Practical updates on tasks completed, current focus, and blockers
- List of immediate dependencies and escalation items
- Responsible owner for each action item
- Required decisions and owners

## 4. ESCALATION PATHWAY

The project will use a clear escalation model to ensure prompt action on risks, issues, or decisions that could affect scope, timeline, cost, or compliance.

### 4.1 Escalation Sequence

| Level | Escalation Trigger | Escalation Path | Expected Response |
|-------|-------------------|----------------|------------------|
| Level 1 | Day-to-day issues / minor delays | Team lead to Project Manager | Within 24 hours |
| Level 2 | Schedule slippage, dependency risk, resource issue | Project Manager to Sponsor / PMO Lead | Within 48 hours |
| Level 3 | Major risk, compliance concern, security event | Project Manager + Sponsor + Compliance/Security lead | Immediate / within 24 hours |
| Level 4 | Budget overrun, material scope change, major go-live risk | Sponsor to Steering Committee / Executive leadership | Immediate decision required |

### 4.2 Decision Authority Matrix

| Decision Type | Decision Owner |
|--------------|---------------|
| Day-to-day task priorities | Project Manager |
| Schedule recovery actions | Project Manager + Sponsor |
| Approved scope changes | Project Sponsor |
| Budget changes above baseline | Executive Sponsor / Steering Committee |
| Security / privacy risk exceptions | Security Lead + Compliance Lead + Sponsor |
| Launch readiness approval | Executive Sponsor / Steering Committee |

## 5. FEEDBACK LOOPS

### 5.1 Feedback Loop Structure
The project will use structured feedback loops to validate requirements, reduce rework, and improve stakeholder confidence.

| Feedback Loop | Purpose | Frequency | Method | Owner |
|---------------|---------|-----------|--------|-------|
| Requirements Review | Validate business needs and acceptance criteria | Biweekly | Workshop / review sessions | Product Manager |
| UX Prototype Feedback | Confirm usability and user journey | Weekly during design | User testing and design review | UX/UI Designer |
| Sprint Retrospective | Improve delivery flow and team coordination | Weekly / per sprint | Team retrospective | Project Manager |
| Risk Review | Monitor threats and corrective actions | Weekly | Risk register update | Project Manager |
| Compliance Review | Validate controls and documentation | As needed / milestone-based | Review and sign-off | Compliance Lead |
| Hypercare Feedback | Support launch issue triage and stabilization | Daily during launch | Operational review | Support Lead / PM |

### 5.2 Feedback Collection Methods
- Stakeholder interviews and workshops
- Prototype and UAT feedback sessions
- Project status meeting input
- Issue tracker updates and risk logs
- Surveys and post-session feedback forms
- Support and operations observations during launch

### 5.3 Feedback Response Mechanism
- Feedback is logged in a central repository
- Items are classified by issue type, urgency, and accountability
- Corrective actions are assigned with deadlines
- Follow-up is communicated to all relevant stakeholders
- Closure criteria are confirmed before closing the feedback item

## 8. GOVERNANCE AND REPORTING RULES

- All stakeholder communications must use a consistent project narrative and terminology.
- All material risks and decisions require documentation in the project log or governance record.
- Schedule and budget variances must be communicated before they become critical path impacts.
- Sponsor and governance decisions must be captured in writing and distributed to the project team.
- No communication should omit the status of scope, risk, decision, and next actions.
