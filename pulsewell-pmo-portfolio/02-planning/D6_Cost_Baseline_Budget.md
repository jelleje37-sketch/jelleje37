## DOCUMENT OVERVIEW

This document defines the approved cost baseline for the PulseWell Digital Intake & Client Experience Portal. The budget includes internal labor, external services, cloud and infrastructure allocations, and a management contingency reserve. The total project budget baseline is set at $41,900.

## 6. MANAGEMENT CONTINGENCY RESERVE

A 10% management contingency reserve is included to account for unplanned scope, schedule risk, technical validation needs, and cost variability during the project lifecycle.

| Reserve Item | Calculation | Amount |
|--------------|------------|--------|
| Management Contingency Reserve | 10% of baseline cost | $4,190 |
| **Total Management Reserve** |  | **$4,190** |

### Contingency Purpose
- Cover additional integration complexity or vendor delays
- Address variances in testing cycles or remediation effort
- Support late-stage compliance review changes or requirement adjustments
- Provide schedule protection for launch readiness activities

> Management reserve is retained centrally and not assigned to specific work packages unless formally approved through change control.

---

## 7. COST BASELINE CONTROL

The cost baseline will be controlled through the following governance approach:

- All project costs must align to approved work packages and budget categories.
- Any cost increase above the approved baseline requires formal change request and sponsor approval.
- Actual costs will be tracked against planned budget throughout the project lifecycle.
- Variance analysis will be reviewed at each project status checkpoint and during milestone reviews.
