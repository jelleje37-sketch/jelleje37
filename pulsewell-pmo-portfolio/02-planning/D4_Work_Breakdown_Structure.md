## DOCUMENT OVERVIEW

This Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) decomposes the PulseWell portal initiative into manageable work packages that align to project scope, planning, execution, and delivery milestones. The WBS provides a structured foundation for estimating effort, assigning ownership, scheduling activities, tracking progress, and coordinating cross-functional dependencies across business, technical, and compliance workstreams.

**WBS Structure:**
- **Level 1:** Project phases and major deliverables
- **Level 2:** Major workstreams and sub-deliverables
- **Level 3:** Detailed work packages and activity groupings

---

## 1. WORK BREAKDOWN STRUCTURE

### 1.0 Project Governance & Management

#### 1.1 Project Initiation & Charter
- 1.1.1 Confirm project scope and business case
- 1.1.2 Define strategic objectives and success metrics
- 1.1.3 Finalize project charter and sponsor approval
- 1.1.4 Establish governance structure and decision rights

#### 1.2 Stakeholder Management
- 1.2.1 Identify internal and external stakeholders
- 1.2.2 Conduct stakeholder analysis and engagement planning
- 1.2.3 Establish communication cadence and governance forums
- 1.2.4 Manage escalation pathways and change approvals

#### 1.3 Planning & Scheduling
- 1.3.1 Develop integrated project schedule
- 1.3.2 Define milestones, dependencies, and critical path
- 1.3.3 Allocate resources and assign responsibilities
- 1.3.4 Baseline scope, schedule, and budget

#### 1.4 Risk, Issue & Change Management
- 1.4.1 Create risk register and mitigation strategy
- 1.4.2 Track issues, decisions, and assumptions
- 1.4.3 Manage change requests and impact assessment
- 1.4.4 Review governance controls and remediation actions

#### 1.5 Quality, Compliance & Reporting
- 1.5.1 Define QA standards and acceptance criteria
- 1.5.2 Monitor privacy, security, and compliance requirements
- 1.5.3 Prepare status reports, dashboards, and executive updates
- 1.5.4 Maintain project documentation and version control

---

### 2.0 Discovery, Requirements & Solution Definition

#### 2.1 Business Requirements Analysis
- 2.1.1 Validate intake and client journey goals
- 2.1.2 Confirm business rules and user personas
- 2.1.3 Prioritize must-have versus future-phase features
- 2.1.4 Produce approved requirements baseline

#### 2.2 User Experience & Research
- 2.2.1 Conduct user interviews and stakeholder workshops
- 2.2.2 Define user journeys and conversion pathways
- 2.2.3 Create UX personas and accessibility requirements
- 2.2.4 Validate proposed experience with prototype testing

#### 2.3 Technical Architecture Definition
- 2.3.1 Define solution architecture and deployment model
- 2.3.2 Assess hosting, security, and scalability needs
- 2.3.3 Document integrations with CRM, billing, and wellness tools
- 2.3.4 Finalize non-functional technical requirements

#### 2.4 Data & Privacy Design
- 2.4.1 Design data model and schema requirements
- 2.4.2 Map data classification and privacy controls
- 2.4.3 Define retention, consent, and audit logging plans
- 2.4.4 Complete privacy impact assessment and controls review

---

### 3.0 Design & Build

#### 3.1 UX/UI Design
- 3.1.1 Develop site map, wireframes, and design system
- 3.1.2 Design client intake and assessment flows
- 3.1.3 Design client dashboard, notifications, and reporting views
- 3.1.4 Produce high-fidelity mockups and design sign-off

#### 3.2 Front-End Development
- 3.2.1 Implement responsive web interface for intake journey
- 3.2.2 Build multi-step form, validation, and progress tracking
- 3.2.3 Develop exercise readiness assessment experience
- 3.2.4 Build dashboard, progress visuals, and recommendations UI
- 3.2.5 Implement accessibility, usability, and browser compatibility checks

#### 3.3 Back-End Development
- 3.3.1 Create application framework and foundational services
- 3.3.2 Design and implement user authentication and RBAC
- 3.3.3 Build client profile, health data, and questionnaire services
- 3.3.4 Develop assessment scoring and recommendation engine
- 3.3.5 Implement audit logging and secure data handling

#### 3.4 Data Management & Integration Development
- 3.4.1 Create database schema and data migration plan
- 3.4.2 Build internal APIs for client and session workflows
- 3.4.3 Develop CRM synchronization logic and error handling
- 3.4.4 Develop billing connector and invoice update process
- 3.4.5 Implement third-party wellness data integration layer

#### 3.5 Security & Compliance Implementation
- 3.5.1 Configure encryption, auth, and session controls
- 3.5.2 Implement role-based access controls and audit trails
- 3.5.3 Validate data retention, consent, and privacy safeguards
- 3.5.4 Complete security testing and remediation across components

#### 3.6 Content & Knowledge Management
- 3.6.1 Prepare help content, FAQ, and user guidance
- 3.6.2 Develop in-app support content and onboarding messaging
- 3.6.3 Create admin documentation and operational procedures
- 3.6.4 Prepare release notes and user-facing education materials

---

### 4.0 Testing, Validation & Readiness

#### 4.1 Functional Testing
- 4.1.1 Execute unit and integration tests for core workflows
- 4.1.2 Validate intake, assessment, scheduling, and dashboard flows
- 4.1.3 Verify business rules and data accuracy against requirements
- 4.1.4 Track defects, retest, and approve resolution

#### 4.2 Non-Functional & Performance Testing
- 4.2.1 Conduct performance, load, and concurrency testing
- 4.2.2 Validate scalability targets and response time thresholds
- 4.2.3 Test accessibility, browser compatibility, and responsiveness
- 4.2.4 Complete security and privacy validation testing

#### 4.3 User Acceptance Testing (UAT)
- 4.3.1 Develop UAT scenarios and test scripts
- 4.3.2 Coordinate business stakeholder validation sessions
- 4.3.3 Record issues and corrective actions
- 4.3.4 Obtain sign-off for release readiness

#### 4.4 Training & Operational Readiness
- 4.4.1 Prepare internal staff training materials
- 4.4.2 Train administrators, support teams, and project champions
- 4.4.3 Validate support procedures and issue triage model
- 4.4.4 Finalize go-live checklist and operational runbook

---

### 5.0 Deployment & Launch

#### 5.1 Release Planning
- 5.1.1 Finalize release scope and deployment schedule
- 5.1.2 Confirm rollback and contingency plans
- 5.1.3 Validate environment readiness and production checks
- 5.1.4 Approve go-live decision by stakeholders

#### 5.2 Production Deployment
- 5.2.1 Configure production infrastructure and security settings
- 5.2.2 Deploy portal and supporting services
- 5.2.3 Execute migration and data validation tasks
- 5.2.4 Monitor platform health and conduct release validation

#### 5.3 Hypercare & Stabilization
- 5.3.1 Monitor client engagement, performance, and issue backlog
- 5.3.2 Provide rapid support and triage during launch period
- 5.3.3 Resolve operational defects and refine workflows
- 5.3.4 Escalate broader risks or service impacts to governance forums

---

### 6.0 Closure & Continuous Improvement

#### 6.1 Project Closure Activities
- 6.1.1 Confirm delivery against requirements and business targets
- 6.1.2 Complete final project status report and governance sign-off
- 6.1.3 Archive project artifacts, documentation, and final records
- 6.1.4 Close risks, issues, and change logs

#### 6.2 Lessons Learned
- 6.2.1 Conduct post-implementation review with stakeholders
- 6.2.2 Capture successes, challenges, and improvement opportunities
- 6.2.3 Document action items for future product phases
- 6.2.4 Align findings to roadmap and backlog planning

## 4. ASSUMPTIONS

- Scope is staged to support an initial release of the PulseWell portal with phased enhancements planned later.
- Cross-functional coordination is required between business, technical, privacy, and operational teams.
- Integration timelines may depend on third-party system availability and API readiness.
- Quality and compliance gates must be met before production deployment.
- A dedicated governance cadence is required for release decisions and risk escalation.
